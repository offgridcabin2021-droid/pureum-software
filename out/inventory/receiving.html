Seamless Receiving.

Close the loop when packages arrive.

Verify Orders

The Receiving tab acts as a checklist for your incoming packages. You see exactly what was ordered (e.g., "Ordered: 2") and can verify against the delivery slip.

Date Tracking

Every pending item has a green pill showing the Ordered Date (e.g., "Ordered: Jan 7, 2026"). This helps you identify late shipments or old backorders instantly.

One-Tap Update

Tap the circle to check an item off, or press the blue "Update" button to confirm the quantity and instantly add it to your live inventory count. No manual math required.

Invoice Handling

The "Select Items for Invoice" bar allows you to batch-process multiple items if a single large shipment arrives, saving you from tapping one by one.
