The Master List.

The central hub for managing your stock.

Item Management

  • Core Actions: Add, edit, and delete inventory items.
  • Smart Search: Find items by name, company, SKU, or category. Search "low" to instantly see low stock items.
  • Filters: Quickly filter by company, category, or stock status.
  • Admin Control: Restricted add/delete permissions for admin users.

Item Details

  • Comprehensive Info: Track Name, SKU, Company (multiple supported), Quantity, and Unit.
  • Location & Category: Organize items by physical location and category.
  • Purchase Tracking: Record purchase prices and add memo notes.
  • Batch Tracking: Manage items with expiry dates and view individual batch details.
  • Stock Threshold: Set custom minimum levels to trigger auto-alerts.

Stock History

  • Full Audit Trail: specific timestamps for every quantity change.
  • Transaction Types: Track increases, decreases, and return transactions (negative quantities).
  • History Filters: Filter history logs to find specific events.

Print Labels & Export

  • Label Generation: Create QR Codes or Barcodes in multiple formats (A4, Avery).
  • Customizable: Choose from small, medium, or large code sizes.
  • Batch Actions: Print labels for single items or in batches.
  • PDF Reports: Generate inventory status reports and email them directly.

Smart Alerts.

Monitor items that need attention.

Alert Management

  • Auto-Generation: Alerts are automatically created when stock drops below thresholds.
  • Status Tracking: Track items across three stages:
    • Needs Order: Low stock, not yet ordered.
    • Backordered: Ordered but partial delivery.
    • Ordered: Fully ordered, awaiting delivery.
  • Manual Alerts: Manually create alerts for any item.

Order Tracking

  • Mark as Ordered: Record the order date and quantity with one tap.
  • Backorder Handling: Track specific backorder quantities.
  • Notes: Add specific notes or instructions to any alert.

Batch Operations

  • Export List: Generate a PDF of all current alerts.
  • Share: Email the order list directly to suppliers or staff.
  • Smart Grouping: Items are grouped by company for efficient ordering from multiple vendors.

Instant Lookup.

Quickly find and update items using the camera.

Scanning Capabilities

  • Universal Support: Scans both QR Codes and standard Barcodes.
  • Enhanced Controls: Start/Stop scanning control and Flashlight toggle for low-light conditions.
  • Manual Lookup: Fallback search by name, company, or legacy UUID if a code is unreadable.

Smart Display

  • Full-Screen View: Immersive camera view with corner guides for easy targeting.
  • Instant Results: Automatically navigates to the item page upon a successful scan.
  • Quick Actions: "Start Scanning" button for rapid-fire inventory checks.

Stock Page.

Manage incoming stock and invoices.

Stock Check List

  • Queue Management: View all ordered items awaiting receipt, grouped by company.
  • Status Filters: Filter by "Ordered" (regular orders) or "Backordered" items.
  • Smart Updates: Search by name or company to quickly find pending items.

Stock & Invoice Management

  • One-Tap Receipt: Quickly update received quantities and expiry dates.
  • Invoice Capture: Take or upload invoice photos. Support for multiple items per invoice.
  • Digital Archive: Invoices are automatically organized by company, year, and month.

Return Items

  • Seamless Returns: Swipe left on stock items to initiate a return.
  • Reason Tracking: Enter quantity and reason to automatically update inventory and history.
  • Stock Adjustment: Automatically decreases inventory and creates a negative stock history entry.

Admin Settings.

Configure the app and view reports.

User & Access Control

  • Role Management: Switch between "Owner" (full access) and "Assistant" (restricted) modes.
  • Team Access: Share a unique clinic code to allow multiple users to join.
  • Security: Admin functions are protected by a secure password.

Annual Archive

  • Year-End Reset: Automatically archive all memos, history, and notes.
  • PDF Records: Download comprehensive PDF reports of past years.
  • Auto-Reminder: Get notified on January 1st to perform the annual cleanup.

Data Management

  • CSV Import/Export: Bulk add items using a CSV template (with AI formatting check).
  • Sample Data: One-tap generic data generation for testing (12, 100, 250, or 500 items).
  • Full Reset: Admin-only option to wipe all cloud and local data.

Label Configuration

  • Format Options: Support for A4 (2x5, 3x6, 4x8) and Avery (5160, 5163, 5167) sheets.
  • Code Types: Choose between QR Code or Barcode.
  • Batch Printing: Select multiple items to generate a single printable PDF.

Reports & Notifications

  • Usage Reports: View monthly and yearly usage trends with top 10 item rankings.
  • Notification Settings: Customize low stock alert frequency and delivery method.
