The Master List.

Total visibility of every item in your clinic.

Smart Filtering

The top bar allows you to filter effectively by brands like 3M, Bausch, or Brasseler. Don't waste time scrolling through thousands of items—tap a tag and see only what you need.

Visual Stock Levels

Each item card clearly displays the current quantity. The units (box, bag, bottle) are prominent so there's no confusion about what "1" means.

Critical Low Stock Alerts

Notice the red highlights on items like "Handpiece Lubricant" and "Single Bond Universal"? When stock dips below your set threshold, the quantity turns red and highlights the item, screaming for attention so you never run out.

Clean Hierarchy

Items are organized with the product name first, followed by the manufacturer. The clean design helps new staff find "Surgical Needles" instantly without needing to know the specific brand code.
