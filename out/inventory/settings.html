Admin Settings.

Manage your clinic and team access.

Role-Based Access

Clearly see your logged-in account (e.g., barebone2016@gmail.com) and your role: Owner. Owners have full access to settings, while Assistants have restricted views to prevent accidental data loss.

Clinic Code Sharing

Your unique Clinic Code (BNG1S5) allows you to easily onboard new staff. Just share the code, and they can join your practice's inventory system instantly.

Notification Control

Toggle Low Stock Notifications on or off. Keep your phone quiet during procedures, or turn it on to ensure you never miss a shortage alert.

Data Management

Export reports, manage database backups, or customize the app's behavior through the collapsible menu sections (Reports, Data Management, Customization).
