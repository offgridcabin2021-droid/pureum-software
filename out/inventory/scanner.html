Instant Lookup.

The fastest way to manage your physical stock.

One-Touch Activation

The blue floating action button is always available. One tap takes you straight to the camera interface, ready to scan.

Visual Guide

The clear blue viewfinder frame guides you to position the QR code or Barcode perfectly. The app automatically recognizes the code effectively, even in low light.

Context-Aware

If you scan an item in the Inventory tab, it opens the detail view for editing. If you scan in the Receiving tab, it automatically checks the item off your delivery list. Smart context means fewer taps.
