Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies specifically to OptiPay, a Canadian tax planning and compensation optimization product offered by Pureum Software.

For broader company legal information, see the Pureum Software Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process

  • Account details such as email and sign-in identifiers
  • Planning inputs such as province, income assumptions, corporate balances, growth targets, and payroll variables
  • User-generated scenarios, optimization outputs, and reports
  • Limited technical and diagnostic data for reliability, security, and support

3. How Information Is Used

  • Calculate salary/dividend strategies, compound growth, and payroll projections
  • Save settings, reports, and preferences across authorized devices when account features are enabled
  • Provide support, improve performance, and protect service integrity

4. Storage, Security, and Sharing

  • OptiPay is designed to minimize data collection and applies standard security controls to transmitted or stored data.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Service providers may be used only when required to operate infrastructure and support under confidentiality obligations.

5. Professional and Regulatory Notice

OptiPay is a planning and calculation tool and does not replace licensed accounting, legal, or financial advice.

Users remain responsible for validating outputs against applicable federal/provincial requirements and their own circumstances.

Where personal information is processed in Canada, privacy practices are intended to align with PIPEDA and relevant provincial rules.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • User-requested account deletion is generally processed within 30 days unless additional retention is required for legal or security reasons.
  • Encrypted backups may persist for up to 90 days before automatic removal.
