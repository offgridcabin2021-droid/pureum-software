Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 | Applies to all Pureum Software products

1. Introduction

Pureum Software ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you how we look after your personal data when you visit our website, use our mobile applications (including PathLog, Doctor Note, OptiPay, PureStock, MoneyPilot, PlantLog, and Retirement Calc Plus), or interact with our services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver exceptional software experiences:

  • Device & Usage Data: Anonymized app telemetry, OS version, and crash logs to improve app stability.
  • User Input & Local Files: Account information, imported CSV files, or KML trail routes are stored locally on your device or secured within authorized cloud backends.
  • Contact Info: Email address provided voluntarily when requesting demos or support.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to provide and optimize our applications, deliver customer support, ensure system security, and comply with legal requirements. We never sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL) for data in transit and robust access controls for data at rest. Offline applications store information securely on your local device enclave.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team at info@pureum.dev.
