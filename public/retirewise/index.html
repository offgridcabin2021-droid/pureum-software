Is RetireWise made specifically for Canadians?+

Yes. Its planning features are designed around Canadian accounts, taxes and retirement benefits, including CPP, OAS, RRSP/RRIF and TFSA considerations.

Can I use it if I own a corporation?+

RetireWise includes corporate owner-manager modeling features for salary, dividends and selected corporate tax accounts.

Does this replace a financial advisor?+

No. RetireWise provides educational estimates and planning tools. Consult a qualified financial, tax or legal professional for advice specific to your circumstances.

Is my information private?+

The product describes a local-first approach without ad tracking, with optional iCloud synchronization. Review its privacy policy for complete details.
