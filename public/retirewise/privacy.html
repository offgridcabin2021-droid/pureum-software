Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Pureum Software

🛡️ Summary in Plain English: RetireWise was architected from line one with absolute privacy as a non-negotiable principle. Your personal financial numbers, corporate account balances (CDA, GRIP, RDTOH), retirement goals, and tax scenario projections are computed on your device. We do not sell your personal data, we do not monetize advertising, and we do not monitor your confidential financial records.

Financial & Tax Modeling Disclaimer:

"Estimates for educational & illustrative purposes only. Not financial, tax, or legal advice. Consult a licensed CFP® or CPA before making financial decisions."

RetireWise calculations, cash flows, and CRA 2026 tax simulations are hypothetical scenario tools. Using this app does not establish a fiduciary or certified advisory relationship with Pureum Software.

1. Who We Are

RetireWise is developed and maintained by Pureum Software ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent digital engineering studio based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your financial simulation privacy, or legal notices, please reach out to us at support@pureum.dev or pureumsoftware@gmail.com.

2. Data Collection and Sovereign Client-Side Architecture

Unlike traditional web-based fintech platforms that demand custody of your online banking credentials and scrape confidential transaction logs, RetireWise operates on a privacy-first, client-side paradigm:

  • Local Financial Simulation Data: All inputs you enter—including age, retirement spending targets, RRSP, TFSA, non-registered balances, CPP/OAS timings, and corporate accounts (CDA, GRIP, RDTOH)—are processed locally on your Apple device.
  • Zero Server-Side Custody: Pureum Software maintains no centralized servers holding your balance sheets or household wealth figures.
  • Optional Encrypted Apple iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud sync to mirror your plans between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac, your data is securely stored within Apple’s encrypted CloudKit private database. Pureum Software holds no cryptographic keys to read or decrypt your private iCloud financial payloads.
  • No Mandatory Account Registration: You do not need to register with a username, password, or provide sensitive identifiers such as your Social Insurance Number (SIN) to use the calculation engine.

3. Financial Modeling Disclaimer & User Responsibility

As detailed across our Terms of Service and app notices:

  • Educational & Illustrative Use: The financial data, retirement trajectories, withdrawal sequences, and tax figures modeled within RetireWise are solely for analytical exploration and self-directed planning.
  • No Professional Advice: Pureum Software does not provide certified financial planning, tax accounting, legal, or investment advisory services. The software does not replace personalized consultation with a licensed Certified Financial Planner (CFP®), Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA), registered tax consultant, or legal attorney.
  • Statutory & Tax Assumptions: Modeling is based on estimated 2026 Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) personal income tax brackets, published standard OAS/CPP formulas, and inflation parameters. Tax legislation and government thresholds change frequently.
  • Hypothetical Returns & Volatility: Compounded investment returns, portfolio growth rates, and sequence of returns projections are hypothetical assumptions; actual market outcomes and personal lifespan will vary.

4. Subscriptions & Apple In-App Purchases

All subscription purchases—including Personal Pro and Advisor Unlimited—are handled directly through Apple’s secure App Store StoreKit infrastructure. Pureum Software never receives or retains your credit card details, banking credentials, or billing address.

Apple provides us solely with anonymized, cryptographic transaction receipts to validate your active subscription entitlement.

5. PDF Report Generation & Document Privacy

When you generate an executive A4 client report or multi-year drawdown summary as a PDF:

  • The PDF file is compiled entirely in memory on your iOS device.
  • It is never uploaded to an external server or third-party cloud render farm.
  • All exported reports carry our standard financial disclaimer: "NOTICE & DISCLAIMER: This planning report is prepared using estimated 2026 CRA personal tax brackets and compound investment returns. This report is for financial modeling purposes and does not constitute formal legal or tax advisory services."
  • You retain 100% control over where you choose to save, print, email, or AirDrop the generated PDF.

6. Diagnostic Logs & Telemetry

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or surveillance analytics SDKs. If you have enabled standard Apple Crash Reporting in your iOS device settings, Apple may share anonymized crash traces with us solely to identify code bugs and improve app stability.

7. Compliance with Canadian Privacy Laws (PIPEDA)

As a Canadian software studio, Pureum Software adheres strictly to the principles of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Because we do not collect, monetize, or profile your personal financial portfolios, your sensitive financial affairs remain strictly under your sovereign control.

8. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of RetireWise after updates signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
