Trust & Financial Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Pureum Software

🛡️ Summary in Plain English: RetireWise was architected from line one with absolute privacy as a non-negotiable principle. Your personal financial numbers, corporate account balances (CDA, GRIP, RDTOH), retirement goals, and tax scenario projections are computed on your device. We do not sell your personal data, we do not monetize advertising, and we do not monitor your confidential financial records.

1. Who We Are

RetireWise is developed and maintained by Pureum Software ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent digital engineering studio based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please reach out to us at support@pureum.dev or pureumsoftware@gmail.com.

2. Data Collection and Storage Architecture

Unlike traditional web-based fintech brokers that demand custody of your banking credentials and scrape your financial history, RetireWise operates on a privacy-first, client-side paradigm:

  • Local Financial Simulation Data: All inputs you enter—including current age, retirement spending targets, RRSP, TFSA, non-registered portfolio balances, and corporate accounts—are processed locally on your Apple device.
  • Optional Encrypted Apple iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud sync to mirror your plans between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac, your data is securely transmitted and stored using Apple’s encrypted CloudKit private database. Pureum Software has no cryptographic keys to read or inspect your private iCloud financial payloads.
  • No Mandatory Account Creation: You do not need to register with a username, password, or provide your Social Insurance Number (SIN) to use the calculation and modeling engine.

3. Subscriptions & Apple In-App Purchases

All subscription purchases—including Personal Pro and Advisor Unlimited—are handled directly through Apple’s secure App Store StoreKit infrastructure. Pureum Software never sees or retains your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials.

Apple provides us solely with anonymized, cryptographic transaction tokens to validate your active subscription tier and grant entitlement to premium features.

4. PDF Report Generation & Document Security

When you export an executive A4 client report or multi-year drawdown summary as a PDF:

  • The PDF file is compiled entirely in memory on your iOS device.
  • It is never uploaded to an external server or third-party cloud render farm.
  • You retain 100% control over where you choose to share, save, or AirDrop the generated PDF.

5. Diagnostic Logs & Telemetry

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers, data brokers, or behavioral analytics SDKs (such as Facebook Pixel or third-party ad networks). If you have enabled standard Apple Crash Reporting in your iOS device settings, Apple may share anonymized crash logs with us solely to identify code defects and improve app stability.

6. Third-Party Services

RetireWise does not connect to external data brokers or open banking scraping networks. External interactions are strictly limited to:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: In-app purchase processing, subscription entitlement validation, and restore purchase flows.
  • Apple CloudKit (Optional): Encrypted private database synchronization between your personal Apple devices under your Apple ID.

7. Compliance with Canadian Privacy Laws (PIPEDA)

As an engineering studio operating in Canada, Pureum Software adheres strictly to the principles set out in the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Because we do not collect, store, or profile your personal financial portfolios on our servers, your personal information remains strictly under your sovereign control.

8. Children's Privacy

RetireWise is intended for adults, working professionals, and business owners planning their long-term retirement strategies. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

9. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of RetireWise after updates signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
