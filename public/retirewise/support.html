Frequently Asked Questions

RetireWise incorporates the comprehensive CRA 2026 Tax Engine. It models combined Federal and Provincial marginal tax brackets across every Canadian province and territory (including Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, and Quebec). It properly differentiates between earned income, eligible Canadian dividends (grossed up with dividend tax credits), non-eligible dividends, capital gains inclusions, and tax-exempt withdrawals (TFSA and corporate CDA).

OAS Protector prioritizes preserving government benefits by keeping taxable net income under the Old Age Security recovery threshold (approx. $90k+). It leans earlier on TFSA and corporate Capital Dividend Account (CDA) distributions.

Wealth Maximizer draws from RRSP/RRIF accounts earlier in retirement to avoid leaving a massive registered balance later in life, preventing the severe "Terminal Tax Cliff" where up to 53% of remaining registered assets can be taxed upon the surviving spouse's death.

Under the Corporate module, you can specify your operating or holding company context: active small business income, passive investment income, and tax account balances such as CDA (Capital Dividend Account), GRIP (General Rate Income Pool), and RDTOH. RetireWise runs multi-iteration simulations to determine the most tax-efficient dividend mix (tax-free capital dividends, eligible, and non-eligible) versus T4 salary for optimal take-home cash.
