A complete operatory workflow.

From low-stock alert to patient chairside, every touchpoint is streamlined.

1. Monitor & Detect

The Inventory Tab serves as your practice command center. Visual color badges immediately identify items below safe thresholds or near expiration.

2. Order & Doctor Sign-Off

Low stock items auto-populate the To Order list. Assistants compile requirements and tap "Email Order List to Doctor" to share professional PDF summaries for one-tap approval.

3. Receive & Archive Invoices

When packages arrive, use Receiving to check in quantities and snap invoice photos. Invoices are automatically organized by supplier and date for effortless accounting.

4. Sterilize & Stay Compliant

Maintain digital sterilization logs with autoclave run numbers, temperatures, and BI spore testing records for peace of mind during health audits.
