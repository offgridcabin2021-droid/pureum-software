A complete workflow.

From stock check to delivery, every step is covered.

1. Monitor

The Inventory Tab is your command center. Use the 'Low' filter to instantly isolate items that need attention. Visual red indicators ensure you never miss a shortage.

2. Order

Found a low item? Add it to your To Order list with one tap. When you're ready, export the list or place the order directly. The 'Ordered' status prevents double-ordering.

3. Receive

When packages arrive, switch to the Receiving Tab. Scan the incoming boxes to instantly update your stock levels and close out the order.
