✧ PureAI Voice & Operatory Assistant

PureAI is PureStock’s native voice and text clinical assistant designed specifically for dental teams. When wearing gloves during patient procedures, clinicians can speak natural dental requests hands-free:

  • "Used 2 carpules of Septanest in Chair 2"
  • "Add 5 boxes of size 2 barrier sleeves to the Henry Schein order"
  • "Check if we have enough Filtek Supreme A2 in stock"
  • "Start an autoclave cycle: Pouches, 30 min, 273°F"

PureAI parses dental terminology and executes native in-app adjustments without requiring screen touches.

Every voice and text action initiated by PureAI presents an interactive Action Confirmation Card with a prominent [↩ Undo] button. If clinical operatory noise causes an inaccurate count, simply tap Undo to immediately reverse the database change.

No. PureStock enforces a strict Zero-PHI architecture. PureStock is purely an operational supply inventory and autoclave compliance platform—never an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system or diagnostic tool. Patient names, health charts, and medical histories are never requested, processed, or saved.

Yes. PureStock differentiates roles between Doctor / Clinic Owner, Associate Dentist, and Dental Assistant / Staff. While staff can record supply usage, log autoclave runs, and request replenishment, sensitive administrative actions (such as permanent item deletions, vendor contract pricing modifications, and staff role changes) require Doctor confirmation.

💳 Subscriptions & Apple In-App Billing

All new clinics receive 7 days of full-featured access across all devices. You will not be billed during the trial. If you choose not to subscribe, simply cancel in your Apple ID Subscriptions settings at least 24 hours before the 7 days elapse.

PureStock is available via Apple In-App Purchase in two tiers:

  • Standard Tier: $19.99 USD / month (or $189.99 USD / year, saving ~21%). Supports up to 300 inventory items, 2 team members, real-time iCloud sync, barcode/QR scanner, purchase order drafts, and core PureAI commands.
  • Premium Tier: $29.99 USD / month (or $289.99 USD / year). Includes unlimited inventory items, unlimited clinic devices, full Autoclave Sterilization & Biological Spore Test logs, delivery invoice photo archiving, and year-end inventory valuation tax exports.

Subscriptions are securely handled by Apple StoreKit. You can manage or cancel anytime directly from your iOS device:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Apple Account / Name at the top.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Choose PureStock and tap Cancel Subscription.

Your access remains active through the end of the paid billing period.

🔐 Account & In-App Data Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), PureStock provides a direct in-app account deletion mechanism under Settings > Account > Delete Practice Data. Confirming this action immediately purges your active inventory, sterilization records, and invoice photos from production servers within 30 days. You may also email pureumsoftware@gmail.com for expedited removal.
