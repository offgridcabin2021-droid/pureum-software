Frequently Asked Questions

🔐 Account & Login

You can create an account using your email, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In. Simply download the app and tap "Sign Up" on the login screen. Follow the prompts to complete your registration.
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use those services to sign in instead.
Important: You must use the same sign-in method you originally registered with. Even if you use the same email address, each sign-in method creates a separate account:
  • Signed up with Google → Must sign in with Google
  • Signed up with Apple → Must sign in with Apple
  • Signed up with Email/Password → Must sign in with Email/Password
Tip: If you can't remember which method you used, try each one. Your inventory data is tied to the sign-in method you originally chose.
Go to Settings > Delete Account within the app. Alternatively, email us at support@pureum.dev to request account deletion. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.
