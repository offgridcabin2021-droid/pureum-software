✨ Denti AI Voice Assistant

Denti AI is PureStock’s native voice and text clinical assistant designed specifically for dental teams. In the middle of patient procedures when wearing gloves, clinicians can simply speak natural requests such as:
  • "Used 2 carpules of Lidocaine in Op 2"
  • "Add 3 boxes of size 2 barrier sleeves to the order list"
  • "Check if we have enough flowable composite A2 in stock"
  • "Start an autoclave cycle: Pouches, 30 min, 121°C"
Denti AI interprets the intent and securely calls native in-app functions to update inventory, create orders, or start cycle timers hands-free.
In full alignment with Apple App Store Guidelines 1.2 & 2.1, every action initiated by Denti AI displays an interactive Action Result Card (ActionResultCard) with a prominent [↩ Undo Last Action] button. If background dental noise caused a transcription inaccuracy or wrong quantity, tap Undo to instantly roll back the database transaction.

💳 Subscriptions & Apple In-App Billing

Eligible new subscribers receive 7 days of complimentary, full-featured access to PureStock. You will not be charged during the trial period. If you decide not to continue, simply cancel at least 24 hours before the 7-day trial ends to avoid being billed.
PureStock offers two straightforward auto-renewable subscription tiers via the Apple App Store:
  • Standard Tier: $19.99 USD / month (or $189.99 USD / year). Includes real-time multi-device cloud sync, barcode/QR scanner, order tracking, invoice archiving, and core Denti AI commands.
  • Premium Tier: $29.99 USD / month (or $289.99 USD / year). Includes all Standard features plus unlimited Denti AI assistant actions, complete Autoclave Sterilization & Biological Indicator (BI) logs, multi-operatory permission controls, and audit trails.
Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple StoreKit. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time using your iOS device:
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Apple Account name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select PureStock and tap Cancel Subscription.
Once canceled, you will continue to have access until the end of your current billing or trial period.

🔐 Account & In-App Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), PureStock provides a direct in-app account deletion mechanism:
  • Open PureStock and tap Settings > Account Deletion.
  • Review the confirmation dialog and confirm deletion.
  • All active inventory items, operatory logs, invoice records, and sterilization cycles are permanently removed from production servers within 30 days. Encrypted backups are completely purged within 90 days.
Alternatively, you can contact support@pureum.dev with your registered email to request manual account termination.
