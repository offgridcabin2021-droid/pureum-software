1. Service Description & License Scope

PureStock is a professional iOS and iPadOS productivity application designed specifically for dental clinics and healthcare practices. The Service delivers:

  • Real-time dental inventory tracking, low-stock threshold alerts, and category shelf management.
  • Supplier purchase order consolidation (Henry Schein, Patterson, Darby, Benco, etc.) and doctor sign-off workflows.
  • Optical code recognition (QR code and 1D/2D barcode scanning) for fast drawer checkouts and stockroom audits.
  • Receiving check-in, backorder tracking, and cloud archiving of paper invoice slips.
  • Autoclave and sterilization cycle logs, biological indicator (BI spore test) tracking, and PDF audit exports.
