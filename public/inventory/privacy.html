PureStock (Inventory App)

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Scope & Application

This Privacy Policy specifically applies to PureStock (Inventory App), a dental clinic inventory and supply management platform developed by Pureum Software.

For corporate-level terms, please see the Pureum Software Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Process

  • Account Credentials: Email address, user ID, clinic membership identifier, and role privileges.
  • Inventory Records: Item titles, SKUs, physical stock quantities, units, storage locations, expiration dates, reorder thresholds, and transaction history.
  • Sterilization & Autoclave Compliance Records: Sterilizer unit identifiers, cycle load numbers, exposure temperatures/durations, biological indicator (BI) spore test pass/fail results, and instrument cycle turnaround dates.
  • Operational Documents: Photos and files of supplier invoices and packing slips voluntarily uploaded by users for clinic record-keeping.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Guideline 5.1.1): When you choose to utilize voice input for Denti AI, the app requests microphone permission (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) and speech recognition permission (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription). Audio data is processed in real time solely to transcribe voice into text prompts via the native iOS Speech framework and ephemeral processing buffers. PureStock never conducts background listening, continuous ambient audio capture, or permanent storage of audio recordings.
  • AI Prompts & Operational Commands: User prompts submitted via text or speech to automate inventory actions.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Crash logs, diagnostic telemetry, and performance metrics required to ensure application stability.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services (Denti AI & Google Gemini)

PureStock incorporates Denti AI to allow users to interact with and manage inventory workflows using natural language prompts.

  • Safe Function Calling (Guideline 2.5.2): Denti AI does not execute arbitrary remote code. It interprets user prompts into structured parameters (JSON) to invoke safe, pre-compiled native functions built directly within the application (e.g., updating stock quantities, adding items, managing alerts).
  • Zero Data Retention Policy: AI prompts are transmitted through secure Firebase Cloud Function proxies to the Google Gemini API. Under commercial enterprise API agreements, customer prompts and inventory records are governed by a strict Zero Data Retention policy and are never used to train or improve underlying AI models.

4. Healthcare & HIPAA Regulatory Notice (US & Canada)

Notice Regarding Healthcare Regulations (HIPAA): PureStock is exclusively a commercial inventory tracking tool designed for dental consumables, supplies, instruments, and equipment. It is not an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system or a medical device.
  • No Protected Health Information (PHI): PureStock does not collect, process, or store patient health information (PHI) such as patient names, chart numbers, health histories, or clinical records. Consequently, HIPAA regulations concerning PHI do not apply to inventory operations within the app.
  • Users and clinics are strictly prohibited from entering patient-identifiable medical records into item names, notes, or memos.
  • We maintain PIPEDA-compliant privacy practices for Canadian practices and adhere to HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification standards where applicable.

5. How Data Is Used

  • Managing dental inventory levels, low-stock notifications, supply reorders, and receiving workflows.
  • Executing requested inventory updates and search functions via Denti AI.
  • Synchronizing inventory records across authorized clinic devices in real time.
  • Providing user support, issue troubleshooting, and continuous service enhancement.

6. Security, Storage & In-App Account Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

  • All data transmissions are encrypted using standard TLS/HTTPS, and cloud data is protected by industry-standard cloud security protocols.
  • In-App Self-Service Deletion: In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), users can permanently delete their user account, credentials, and associated clinic data directly within the iOS app by visiting Settings > Account Deletion.
  • Upon confirmed deletion, authentication access is immediately terminated, active cloud production records are permanently expunged within 30 days, and encrypted system backups are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Access to production infrastructure is strictly restricted based on role and operational necessity.

3. AI 처리 및 서드파티 AI 정책 (Denti AI & Google Gemini)

PureStock은 프롬프트를 통해 재고 업무를 빠르고 간편하게 처리할 수 있도록 Denti AI 기능을 지원합니다.

  • 안전한 함수 호출 (Function Calling / Apple 지침 2.5.2 준수): 외부에서 임의의 실행 코드를 동적으로 다운로드받아 실행하지 않습니다. AI는 사용자의 요청을 분석하여 매개변수(JSON)만 생성하며, 앱 내부에 미리 안전하게 빌드된 네이티브 함수(예: 재고 수량 수정, 품목 추가, 주문 알림 등록 등)만을 실행합니다.
  • 데이터 미저장 정책 (Zero Data Retention): AI 요청은 보안 Firebase Cloud Function 프록시를 통해 Google Gemini API로 전송됩니다. 상용 API 보안 정책에 따라 고객의 재고 데이터 및 프롬프트는 AI 모델 학습에 일절 활용되지 않으며, 데이터 미저장(Zero Data Retention) 원칙이 철저하게 보장됩니다.
