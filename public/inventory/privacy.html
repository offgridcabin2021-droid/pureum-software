Practice Commitment: PureStock is engineered exclusively for dental supply inventory, supplier replenishment, and autoclave sterilization tracking. PureStock is not an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system and strictly prohibits the storage of Protected Health Information (PHI).

1. Scope & Application

This Privacy Policy explains how Pureum Software ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when dental practices, clinicians, practice owners, and authorized staff members ("Users" or "Practice") access and use the PureStock iOS mobile application, iPad application, and related cloud synchronization services (collectively, the "Service").

By downloading, installing, registering for, or using PureStock, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not access or use the Service.

2. Data We Process

We process only the minimum operational data necessary to deliver smart inventory tracking, vendor reordering, and clinical compliance records:

A. Practice Inventory & Supply Data

  • Dental supply names, manufacturer details, model numbers, and internal catalog codes (e.g., 3M Filtek, Septodont Septanest).
  • Quantities on hand, minimum reorder thresholds, unit costs, and storage locations (operatory drawers, central stockrooms).
  • Batch numbers, lot identifiers, and expiration dates for FIFO (First-In, First-Out) shelf management.

B. Purchasing & Invoices

  • Vendor names (e.g., Henry Schein, Patterson Dental, Darby, Benco), order line items, order status, and doctor review timestamps.
  • Photographic captures of paper delivery slips and vendor invoices captured using the device camera for internal practice verification.

C. Autoclave & Sterilization Compliance Logs

  • Autoclave machine identifiers, cycle run numbers, cycle dates, temperature/pressure readings, and technician operator names/signatures.
  • Biological Indicator (BI spore test) results, incubation monitoring records, and pass/fail indicators.

D. Account & Device Information

  • Practice name, clinic administrator email address, authentication credentials, and assigned role permissions (Owner, Associate Doctor, Assistant).
  • Basic diagnostic device tokens, iOS version, and crash diagnostic logs to maintain sync stability.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services (PureAI & Google Gemini)

PureStock incorporates PureAI, an intelligent assistive capability powered by the Google Gemini API, allowing hands-free voice and natural language text commands in the operatory:

  • Strictly Operational Queries: PureAI only processes queries related to dental supplies (e.g., "Add 10 boxes of composite resin A2 to the Henry Schein reorder list" or "Check how many vials of lidocaine are left").
  • No Model Training: Your clinic's inventory data, operational records, and voice queries are never used to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundation AI models.
  • Zero PHI Transmission: Users are expressly prohibited from entering patient names, charts, treatment plans, or health records into PureAI prompts.
  • Explicit Action Confirmation: PureAI prompts show clear preview cards before modifying any stock counts or submitting purchase orders, with full undo capability.

4. Healthcare & HIPAA Regulatory Notice (US & Canada)

Non-Medical, Non-EHR Operational Software: PureStock is not a medical device, diagnostic system, or Electronic Health Record (EHR) repository under HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) or PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act).

PureStock is strictly an operational business tool designed to manage clinic supplies, vendor procurement, and autoclave equipment maintenance. PureStock does not collect, solicit, or store Protected Health Information (PHI) or personal patient healthcare records.

The Practice remains solely responsible for ensuring that staff members do not input patient health information into item notes, photo attachments, or AI prompts.

5. How Practice Data Is Used

We use the processed data solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Enabling real-time cloud synchronization across all authenticated iOS devices in your practice.
  • Calculating automated low-stock warnings and building supplier-grouped purchase order sheets.
  • Generating exportable PDF compliance logs for state dental board and health authority inspections.
  • Creating year-end inventory valuation and tax-readiness reports for practice accounting.
  • Providing technical customer support and maintaining platform reliability.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade practice operational data to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

6. Security, Storage & In-App Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your practice data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your iOS devices and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3), and stored in secured cloud databases with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Practice owners can restrict staff permissions so only authorized personnel can view pricing, approve purchase orders, or alter audit logs.

Direct In-App Account Deletion

In strict accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), PureStock provides an automated, instantaneous, and direct in-app account deletion mechanism:

  • The Practice Administrator can initiate permanent account deletion at any time by navigating to: PureStock App > Settings > Account > Delete Account & Erase All Practice Data.
  • Upon confirmation, your account authentication credentials, active inventory master lists, purchase order history, sterilization logs, uploaded invoice photos, and device sync tokens are permanently and irreversibly purged from our active databases and cloud storage without requiring you to email support or wait for manual intervention.
