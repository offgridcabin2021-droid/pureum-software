Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies specifically to Inventory App, a dental inventory management product by Pureum Software.

For company-wide legal terms, see Pureum Software Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Process

  • Account data (email, sign-in identifiers)
  • Inventory records (item name, quantity, unit, location, category)
  • Operational documents (for example invoice images uploaded by users)
  • Usage data required for app performance and reliability

3. How Data Is Used

  • Manage inventory, low-stock alerts, ordering, and receiving workflows
  • Enable sync and backup across authorized devices
  • Provide user support, troubleshooting, and service improvements

4. Security and Retention

  • Data is transmitted and stored using standard cloud security controls.
  • Upon account deletion request, data is deleted within 30 days; backups may persist up to 90 days.
  • Access to production systems is limited by role and operational need.

5. Healthcare Notice (US/Canada)

We apply HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification standards where applicable.

We maintain PIPEDA-compliant privacy practices for the collection, use, disclosure, and retention of personal information.

Do not store full patient medical records in Inventory App unless your organization has a separate legal basis and documented compliance process.
