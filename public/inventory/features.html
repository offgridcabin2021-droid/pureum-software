Denti AI Operatory Assistant.

Hands-free voice recognition, natural language replenishment, and role-enforced safety guardrails.

Hands-Free Voice Flow in the Operatory

  • Gloved Hands Friendly: Tap the microphone button (🎙️) and speak naturally without having to touch device screens or remove clinical PPE.
  • Dental Lexicon Tuned: Understands clinical terminology, brand names (3M, Kerr, Dentsply, NSK, Terumo), gauge sizes, and packaging units.
  • Multilingual Support: Query and instruct in English, Korean, or mixed bilingual phrases without configuration friction.

Context-Aware Tab Commands

  • Inventory Screen: “Give me a clinic briefing”, “Where is Lidocaine and how many are left?”, “We used 3 boxes of 27G needles”.
  • To Order Screen: “Add low stock items to To Order”, “Show backordered items”, “Email To Order list to Doctor”.
  • Receiving Screen: “Received 5 boxes of composites”, “Show recent invoices and audit history”.
  • Scanner Screen: “Show items running low while scanning”, “Add item to reorder list”.

Clinical Role Safeguards & Undo Engine

  • Doctor vs. Assistant Hierarchy: Assistants can record stock usage, check deliveries, and draft orders. Only verified Doctors/Admins can finalize purchase orders, edit clinic valuations, or delete records.
  • Visual Action Result Cards: Every action executed by Denti AI displays an immediate structured confirmation card with exact quantities and timestamps.
  • One-Tap Instant Undo: Made a misstatement? Tap [↩ Undo Last Action] to instantly reverse the last inventory update or queue change.

Compliance, Safety & Zero PHI Architecture

  • Apple Guideline 2.5.2 Compliance: Denti AI never downloads or runs dynamic arbitrary remote code. It maps language into structured parameters to call safe, pre-compiled native Swift methods.
  • Zero Data Retention: Cloud inference proxies to enterprise APIs operate under strict Zero Data Retention agreements. Clinic prompts are never used for model training.
  • Strictly Non-PHI: PureStock tracks dental equipment and materials only. No patient health records or charts are collected, stored, or required.

The Master List.

Complete visibility and control across every operatory and storage cabinet.

Item Management & Organization

  • Core Records: Track Name, SKU, Supplier/Company (multiple supported), Quantity, Unit, Category, and Storage Location.
  • Color-Coded Status Dots: Green for adequate stock, Amber for low-stock threshold alerts, and Red for items near expiration.
  • Smart Search & Fast Filters: Instantly search by name or brand, or type low to isolate items requiring attention.
  • Integrated Denti AI: Launch the intelligent assistant right from the search bar with one tap.

Batch & Lot Number Tracking (FIFO)

  • Expiry Monitoring: Assign expiration dates to individual batches for dental bonding agents, anesthetics, and impression materials.
  • FIFO Usage: Enforce First-In-First-Out dispensing to eliminate expired stock waste in operatory drawers.
  • Purchase Pricing & Memos: Track unit purchase costs and leave handover notes for clinic staff.

Audit Trail & Label Generation

  • Complete Stock History: Detailed timestamped logs of every increase, decrease, or return transaction with user identification.
  • Print QR / Barcode Labels: Generate printable labels compatible with A4 (2x5, 3x6, 4x8) and Avery (5160, 5163, 5167) sheet formats.
  • PDF Inventory Reports: Export comprehensive stock reports and valuations directly from the app.

Automated Replenishment.

Proactive shortage detection and streamlined doctor order approvals.

Automated Threshold Alerts

  • Zero Stockouts: When stock dips below your customized minimum threshold, items are automatically queued into the 'To Order' list.
  • Three-Stage Pipeline:
    • Needs Order (Orange): Items awaiting order placement.
    • Ordered (Green): Order submitted to dental supplier.
    • Backordered (Red): Partially fulfilled or supplier-delayed shipments.

Doctor Collaboration & PDF Export

  • Email Order List to Doctor: Assistants can compile required orders and generate a clean PDF summary with one tap, opening the native iOS share sheet.
  • Grouped by Dental Supplier: Items are organized by company (Henry Schein, Patterson Dental, Benco, etc.) for rapid ordering.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Clear status tagging prevents multiple assistants from double-ordering the same material.

Rapid Camera Scanner.

High-speed barcode & QR recognition designed for fast clinic inventory counts.

Instant Optical Recognition

  • Universal Format Support: Seamlessly decodes QR codes, Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13, and UPC dental manufacturer barcodes.
  • Low-Light Operatory Torch: Built-in flashlight toggle allows effortless scanning inside dark storage cabinets and under-counter shelves.
  • Direct Item Lookup: Automatically navigates to the item detail screen with full location and stock history upon scanning.

Configurable Quick Scan Actions

  • Rapid +/-1 Mode: Set scan action to automatically deduct (-1) when taking an item into an operatory, or add (+1) when unpacking shipments.
  • Scan Undo Safeguard: An immediate Undo button appears following every scan to protect against accidental double-scans.

Receiving & Invoice Archive.

Check in supplier deliveries and organize photographic invoice records by date.

Delivery Check-In & Reconciliation

  • Stock Check List: View all items currently pending delivery grouped by dental supplier.
  • One-Tap Quantity Receipt: Confirm full shipments or enter partial quantities for backordered items.
  • Automated Stock Updates: Receiving items automatically updates the inventory master list and records an audit log entry.

Photographic Invoice Management

  • Snap Paper Invoices: Capture packing slips and invoices with your camera upon unpacking boxes.
  • Organized by Supplier & Month: Invoices are automatically filed by supplier, year, and month for frictionless bookkeeper and accountant access.
  • Return Item Workflow: Swipe left to record supplier returns, capturing return reasons and generating credit records.
🔬 Clinical Compliance Module

Sterilization & Autoclave Logs.

Digital compliance tracking, biological indicator test records, and instrument cycle monitoring.

Digital Autoclave Cycle Logging

  • Cycle Parameter Records: Record sterilizer unit ID, cycle date/time, load number, target temperature, and sterilization duration.
  • Biological Indicator (BI) Spore Tests: Log daily/weekly spore test vials with incubation start/end times and clear Pass/Fail readings.
  • Chemical Indicator Verification: Document Type 5/6 integrators inside cassettes and pouch loads for complete sterilization assurance.

Instrument Turnover & Overdue Alerts

  • Cycle Turnaround Tracking: Set maximum shelf life or cycle frequency for sterilized instrument cassettes and surgical kits.
  • Overdue Warnings: Denti AI and visual indicators notify staff when instruments require re-sterilization prior to scheduled procedures.
  • Inspection-Ready Audit Export: Generate digital logs in PDF format ready to present to public health and dental regulatory inspectors.

Practice Settings & Security Roles.

Role-based clinic permissions, multi-device team syncing, and annual archives.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  • Doctor / Owner Role: Full administrative privileges, item pricing & inventory valuation, supplier deletion, and order confirmation.
  • Assistant / Staff Role: Operatory usage recording, delivery check-ins, To Order queuing, notes, and sterilization logging.
  • Clinic Code Sharing: Invite team members securely via unique 6-character clinic pairing codes.

Annual Archive & Year-End Reset

  • Permanent Audit Records: Archive all historical stock movements, memos, and invoice logs into encrypted annual PDF archives.
  • Clean Slate for New Year: Archive previous year records while preserving active inventory quantities and supplier catalogues.
  • Automated Reminder: System notification prompts staff to perform the annual archive on January 1st.

Bulk CSV Import & Self-Service Deletion

  • CSV Template Import: Bulk upload existing clinic inventory spreadsheets with built-in format verification.
  • Self-Service Account Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data directly within the app under Settings.
