Mindful Botanics

Designed for Flourishing Plants

Every detail is crafted with care — combining native iOS elegance with thoughtful plant care utility.

Smart Local Push Reminders

Receive watering alerts at your preferred morning or evening hour. If today is too busy, tap "Remind me tomorrow" to snooze without breaking your routine.

Custom Watering Cadence

Cacti need monthly attention while ferns need weekly care. Define precise interval days for each specimen and track their real-time hydration state: Hydrated, Thirsty, or Overdue.

Photo Log & Growth Tracking

Document your botanical journey with photos, nicknames, and adoption days. Watch how your monstera or fiddle leaf fig thrives over the seasons.

Room & Location Sorting

Organize by rooms (Living Room, Kitchen, Balcony, Bedroom, Office) or define custom locations. Never wonder which corner of the house needs watering next.

Absolute Data Sovereignty

Your photos and notes never touch our servers. Stored natively on your device via SwiftData with zero advertising, zero trackers, and zero telemetry.

Single-File Backup & Transfer

Export all plants, history, and photos into a portable .plantlog file. Seamlessly AirDrop or sync to another device whenever you upgrade your phone.
