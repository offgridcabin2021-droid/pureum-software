Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026 • Pureum Software

🌱 Summary in Plain English: PlantLog is built with privacy at its foundation. Your plant photos, watering schedules, and notes are stored 100% locally on your iOS device. We do not track you, we do not require account registration, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

Pureum Software ("we", "us", or "our") develops PlantLog to make indoor and garden plant care simple, joyful, and completely private. We respect your personal privacy and adhere to the principle of data minimization: we do not collect what we do not need.

2. Data Collection and Storage

All information you create and manage in PlantLog is kept locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Plant Profiles: Plant names, species, nicknames, locations (e.g., Living Room, Balcony), and adoption dates.
  • Watering Logs: Dates watered, overdue statuses, custom intervals, and scheduling preferences.
  • Photos: Any images you attach to your plants remain exclusively in your local storage sandbox and are never uploaded to our servers.

Because we do not operate user accounts, we do not have access to your database or images. If you delete the app or lose your device without an iOS system backup or a .plantlog export, your data cannot be retrieved by us.

3. Device Permissions We Request

PlantLog requests the following iOS system permissions solely to deliver features you choose to use:

  • Notifications (UserNotifications): Needed to send you scheduled watering reminders and snooze alerts at your designated time. Reminders are generated locally by your device.
  • Photo Library / Camera: Optional permission requested only when you choose to take or select a photo for a plant profile.

4. Data Backup and Export

PlantLog provides a native Data Transfer feature in Settings:

  • Export: Allows you to generate a secure, encrypted .plantlog backup package containing your plant records and photos. You choose where to store or send this file (e.g., Files app, AirDrop, personal iCloud Drive).
  • Import: Restores your full collection from a previously saved .plantlog file.

5. Analytics, Tracking & Advertising

PlantLog is completely free of third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, and profiling tools. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites, and we comply fully with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

6. In-App Support Tips (StoreKit)

If you choose to support PlantLog's development via optional tips (In-App Purchases), Apple processes the payment transaction securely through the App Store. We receive only non-identifying transaction verification receipts necessary to confirm your support. We never receive or store your payment card numbers.

7. Children's Privacy

PlantLog does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
