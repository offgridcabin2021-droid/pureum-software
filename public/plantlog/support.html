Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I not receiving watering reminder notifications?

First, ensure notifications are allowed in your device settings under iOS Settings > Notifications > PlantLog. Inside the app, confirm that Reminders are toggled ON in the plant's profile and check your designated reminder hour. Also check if iOS Focus (Do Not Disturb) is silencing banner alerts.

How do I transfer my plants and history to a new iPhone?

Open Settings > Data Transfer > Export Data in PlantLog. This creates a single, self-contained .plantlog backup file containing all your plant records, photos, and watering logs. Save this file to personal iCloud Drive, AirDrop it to your new phone, then tap Import Data in the new app to restore everything instantly.

Can I postpone or snooze a watering alert?

Yes! On the Today view, swipe or open the options menu for any plant due today and tap "Remind me tomorrow" or Snooze. PlantLog will delay the reminder without resetting your plant's natural watering cycle history.

Where is my data stored? Do you collect my plant photos?

All data is stored 100% on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate external databases, we never upload your plant photos, and we do not track your activity. Your botanical journal is completely private to you.

Is PlantLog completely free? What are optional tips?

PlantLog is completely free, ad-free, and unrestricted. We offer optional support tips in the Settings tab (Coffee Tip, Small Tip, Garden Tip) for users who want to contribute towards independent craft development. Tips do not lock or unlock features.
